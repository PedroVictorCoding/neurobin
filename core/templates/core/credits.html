🧬 Scientific Databases & APIs

ChEMBL Database

European Bioinformatics Institute (EMBL-EBI)

A large-scale bioactivity database containing binding, functional and ADMET information for a large number of drug-like bioactive compounds.

Used for: Compound data, target information, bioactivity data, mechanisms of action

License: CC BY-SA 3.0

Visit ChEMBL

Reactome Pathway Database

Reactome Consortium

A free, open-source, curated and peer-reviewed pathway database providing intuitive bioinformatics tools for the visualization and analysis of pathway data.

Used for: Biological pathways, protein interactions, molecular mechanisms

License: CC BY 4.0

Visit Reactome

PubChem

National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI)

A database of chemical molecules and their activities against biological assays, maintained by the National Institutes of Health.

Used for: Chemical structures, molecular properties, compound validation

License: Public Domain

Visit PubChem

UniProt

UniProt Consortium (EBI, SIB, PIR)

A comprehensive, high-quality and freely accessible database of protein sequence and functional information.

Used for: Protein information, gene names, functional annotations

License: CC BY 4.0

Visit UniProt

📜 Licensing & Compliance

Data Usage Compliance

Neurobin respects all licensing terms and usage policies of the data sources we integrate. All data is used in accordance with the respective licenses:

  • ChEMBL: CC BY-SA 3.0 - Attribution required
  • Reactome: CC BY 4.0 - Attribution required
  • PubChem: Public Domain - No restrictions
  • UniProt: CC BY 4.0 - Attribution required

When using Neurobin data derived from these sources, please ensure proper attribution to the original databases.

Research Use

Neurobin is designed for educational and research purposes. Please consult with qualified professionals for any medical, therapeutic, or commercial applications.
